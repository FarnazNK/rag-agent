# New Hire Onboarding

The first 30 days at Acme Corp are structured around three goals: get set up,
meet your team, and ship your first contribution.

## Day 1
- IT setup with the People Ops team
- Sign benefits enrollment forms (must be completed within 30 days)
- Receive laptop, badge, and Slack access
- Lunch with onboarding buddy

## Week 1
- Complete required compliance training (security, harassment prevention,
  data privacy) — all available in the LMS
- 1:1 with manager to review the 30/60/90 plan
- Shadow at least 2 cross-functional partners

## Week 2–4
- Begin work on a starter project agreed with manager
- Attend new hire orientation (held the second Tuesday of every month)
- Schedule coffee chats with at least 5 colleagues outside your immediate team

## 30-day check-in
At the 30-day mark, the new hire and manager complete a structured check-in
covering ramp-up, blockers, and any benefits or logistics issues. HR Business
Partner joins if escalation is needed.

## Equipment
Standard equipment package: 16" MacBook Pro, external monitor, keyboard, mouse,
headset. Engineers may request a Linux workstation through their manager.
