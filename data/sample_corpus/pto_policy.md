# Paid Time Off Policy

All full-time employees at Acme Corp accrue paid time off (PTO) on a monthly basis.

## Accrual rate
- 0–2 years of tenure: 15 days per year (1.25 days/month)
- 2–5 years of tenure: 20 days per year (1.67 days/month)
- 5+ years of tenure: 25 days per year (2.08 days/month)

## Carryover
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year.
Any balance above 5 days as of December 31 is forfeited.

## Requesting time off
Submit PTO requests through Workday at least 2 weeks in advance for any block
longer than 2 consecutive days. Manager approval is required.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO and accrues at 1 day per month, capped at
10 days. Sick leave does not roll over.
