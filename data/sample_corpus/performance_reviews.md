# Performance Review Cycle

Acme Corp runs two formal performance review cycles per year: H1 (January–June)
and H2 (July–December). Reviews are completed in the month following each cycle.

## Components
1. **Self-review** — employees rate themselves against role expectations and
   list top accomplishments and areas for growth.
2. **Manager review** — direct manager assesses performance using the same
   rubric and writes development feedback.
3. **Peer reviews** — 3–5 peers selected jointly by employee and manager.
4. **Calibration** — managers across each org meet to normalize ratings and
   surface promotion candidates.

## Rating scale
- Exceeds expectations
- Meets expectations
- Developing
- Below expectations

## Promotion criteria
Promotion to the next level requires:
- Two consecutive cycles at "Exceeds expectations" OR strong calibration
  support for level-up scope.
- Demonstrated impact at the next level for at least one quarter.
- No active performance improvement plan (PIP).

## Compensation adjustments
Merit increases and equity refreshes are decided at the end of each H2 cycle
and take effect on April 1 of the following year.
